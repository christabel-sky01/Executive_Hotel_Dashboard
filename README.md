# Executive_Hotel_Dashboard
Executive Hotel Performance Dashboard | Power BI analytics for hotel KPIs, revenue, occupancy, and business insights.


## 📌 Project Overview

The Executive Hotel Performance Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze hotel performance, revenue, booking trends, cancellations, and customer demographics.

The dashboard transforms hotel booking data into meaningful visual insights to support data-driven decision-making and help management monitor key business performance indicators.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor hotel revenue and booking performance.
- Analyze confirmed, cancelled, and pending bookings.
- Track booking trends over time.
- Compare revenue performance across hotels.
- Explore booking distribution by country.
- Understand customer demographics by age group and gender.
- Present key performance indicators in an interactive dashboard.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query** — data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX** — calculated measures and key performance indicators.
- **Data Analytics** — exploratory analysis and business reporting.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Realized Revenue
- Confirmed Occupancy Rate
- Total Bookings
- Cancellation Rate

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- Executive KPI cards for quick performance monitoring.
- Monthly booking trend analysis.
- Booking status distribution.
- Hotel revenue comparison.
- Geographic analysis of bookings by country.
- Customer demographic analysis.
- Interactive filters for city, room type, and customer type.

## 💡 Business Questions

This project explores questions such as:

1. How much revenue has the hotel business generated?
2. How many bookings were made, confirmed, cancelled, or left pending?
3. How do booking volumes change over time?
4. Which hotels generate the highest revenue?
5. Which countries contribute the most bookings?
6. What customer demographic patterns appear in the data?

## 📌 Business Value

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of hotel performance, helping stakeholders monitor KPIs, explore booking patterns, investigate cancellations, compare hotel revenue, and identify opportunities for operational improvement.

## 💼 Recommendations

- Investigate cancellation patterns to identify possible causes and improve reservation retention.
- Monitor monthly booking trends to support staffing and resource planning.
- Compare hotel revenue performance to identify areas requiring further investigation.
- Use customer demographic insights to inform targeted marketing strategies.
- Validate KPI calculations and investigate unexpected values before making business decisions.
